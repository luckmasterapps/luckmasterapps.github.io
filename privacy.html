Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

WinCheck USA ("WinCheck", "we", "us") is an app that shows US lottery results and checks lottery tickets for you. WinCheck USA is published by Luck master. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be read, so it is short where we can make it short.

The short version

  • You don't need an account. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or payment details.
  • Your tickets, your numbers and your choices stay on your phone.
  • A photo of a ticket is read on your phone and is not uploaded or saved.
  • We don't sell your information and we don't show ads.
  • We use a few Google services and a cloud hosting provider to run the app. They are listed below.

Information the app uses

Information you give the app

  • Lotteries and settings. The lotteries you follow, your home state, the layout of your Home screen and similar choices. They are stored on your phone only.
  • Tickets. The game, draw date and numbers of the tickets you type in, scan or save, and whether you marked a prize as claimed. They are stored on your phone only. We cannot see them.
  • Feedback you send us. If you use Settings › Send feedback, we receive your message, the kind of feedback you chose, your email address only if you typed one, and technical details about your phone and the app (phone model, Android version, app version, screen size, language, time zone, the home state you chose in Settings, memory, and whether you were on Wi‑Fi or mobile data). We do not collect your name, phone number, advertising ID, location or contacts this way. They are stored in Google Firestore and used only to read and answer your message. If you email us for support, we receive your email address and what you write.

Camera and photos

  • When you scan a ticket, the app uses your camera to read the text on it. The text is recognized on your phone by Google ML Kit (the model is part of the app). The photo is not uploaded, not saved to your gallery and not shared. It is kept in memory only until you have checked the ticket or left the scan screen.
  • When you choose photos from your gallery (up to 3), the app uses the Android photo picker. The app only receives the photos you pick. It does not get access to the rest of your photos and does not ask for storage permission.
  • You can use WinCheck without the camera by typing your numbers.

Information collected automatically

  • Lottery data requests. To show results, the app downloads lottery data files from our servers (hosted by a cloud hosting provider). Like any internet request, this shows your IP address and basic device and app details (such as app version and Android version) to the server. The requests do not contain your tickets or your choices.
  • Diagnostics and usage. We use Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports and Firebase Analytics to understand which screens are used and where people get stuck (for example, whether a ticket scan succeeded). These services receive an app installation identifier, device model, operating system version, app version, country, crash traces and the events listed. They do not receive your ticket numbers, photos or the text of your messages.
  • Notifications. If you turn on alerts, Firebase Cloud Messaging gives your phone a token so we can send you a notification (for example, that results are posted). The token is tied to the app on your phone, not to your name.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your precise or approximate location, contacts, calendar, microphone recordings, files, financial information, health information or government identifiers. WinCheck does not use advertising identifiers.

How we use information

  • To show lottery results, schedules and prizes and to keep them up to date.
  • To check your tickets against official results and tell you what you won.
  • To send alerts you asked for.
  • To find and fix crashes and bugs and to improve the app.
  • To answer your questions and to protect the app against misuse.
  • To follow the law.

Who receives information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We use these service providers, who process information only to provide their service to us:

  • Google (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Messaging; ML Kit). See Google's privacy information at policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Our cloud hosting provider hosts the lottery data files and receives the data requests described above.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or as part of a sale or merger of our business (in which case this policy continues to apply until you are told otherwise).

Where your information is stored

Tickets and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app or choosing Clear storage in Android settings removes them. Because we cannot see them, we cannot restore them if you lose them. Diagnostic and analytics data is processed on Google's servers, which may be in the United States and other countries.

How long we keep information

  • Tickets and settings: until you delete them, uninstall the app or clear its storage. Tickets that you checked but did not save are removed automatically after one day.
  • Crash reports and analytics: kept by Google for the period set in our Firebase project (up to 14 months for analytics events, 90 days for crash reports), then deleted.
  • Feedback messages and support emails: for as long as needed to help you and then for up to 24 months.

Your choices

  • Permissions. You can turn the camera and notifications on or off at any time in Android Settings → Apps → WinCheck USA → Permissions.
  • Analytics and diagnostics. You can turn off usage statistics and crash reports at any time in Settings › Usage data and crash reports.
  • Advertising ID. The app does not use it.
  • Delete your data. Delete tickets inside the app, or uninstall the app. To ask us to delete anything we hold about you (for example a support email), write to luckmasterapps@gmail.com.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas and other US states, or the UK and EU), you may have the right to know what personal information we hold, to access, correct or delete it, to receive a copy, and to object to or limit some uses. We do not sell or "share" personal information as those laws define the terms, and we do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you. To use your rights, email luckmasterapps@gmail.com. We will not treat you differently for using them. If we cannot help, you can contact your state attorney general or data protection authority.

Because most of what the app stores is only on your phone, we usually can't link anything to you. We may ask you for details that help us find the information you ask about.

Children

WinCheck USA is for adults. Lottery tickets can only be bought by people who are at least 18 (or 19, 21 or older in some states). The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, email luckmasterapps@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Tickets and settings are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on an unmodified phone. No method of storing or sending data is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Third-party links and content

The app may open links to official lottery websites. They have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for them. WinCheck USA is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any state lottery, Powerball, Mega Millions or any other lottery operator.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app uses information, we will update this policy and the date at the top. If a change is important (for example, if we add ads or accounts), we will tell you in the app before it starts.
